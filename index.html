About

I operate at the intersection of structured analysis and emotional depth — building business intelligence systems by day and writing psychological fiction by night.

At Biopolis Lifesciences, I lead MIS reporting and operational strategy across a multi-company group, managing a team of 108. My work involves turning raw business data into dashboards, decision systems, and actionable insight — reducing manual reporting effort by 80% through automation.

Outside of work, I write under the pen name Poker Hearts. My published books — Light Enough and Hi Heart I Read — explore introspective narratives. I'm currently developing Sessions, a psychological thriller centered on memory distortion and therapeutic deconstruction.

I hold an MBA in Financial Markets from Mittal School of Business & NSE Academy (8.83 CGPA), a BBA (Hons) foundation (8.36 CGPA), am UGC NET Qualified in Management, and hold 9+ NCFM certifications spanning financial modelling, technical analysis, and derivatives.
