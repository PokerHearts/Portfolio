01 / Group Biopolis · Dual Distribution Enterprise

18-Module Internal Decision Platform

18 Custom Modules
80 FTEs Active Scope
Zero Consulting Drag

Fragmented, manual spreadsheet entries across dual pharmaceutical distribution companies caused significant operational latency, delayed monthend reconciliations, and obscured daily cash flow.

Directed the end-to-end architecture and company-wide rollout of an 18-module internal decision platform. Personally engineered custom Google Apps Script ingestion engines, a Manifest V3 Chrome Extension bypassing Looker Studio's 15-minute caching latency, and automated reconciliation pipelines for attendance, inventory, and field sales bookings.

  • B2B Digital Order Pad: Replaced scattered phone and messaging orders with a single-page order book, slashing booking loops from 8 minutes down to 45 seconds.
  • Manifest V3 Chrome Extension: Injected background event runners to trigger real-time, non-invasive Looker Studio dashboard refreshes during monthend executive sales closings.
  • Organizational Impact: Eliminated 40+ hours of weekly clerical friction and achieved 100% voluntary adoption across 30+ non-technical field and warehouse personnel.
02 / Bharat Financial Inclusion · IndusInd Bank Subsidiary

Operational Turnaround & Quota Sprint

72 Team Members
10 Regional Hubs
48h Sprint Velocity

Diagnosed systemic operational inertia across a 72-member field operations division managing 7,500+ microfinance borrower accounts across 10 regional territories. Monthly targets were routinely backloaded to the final days, causing extreme operational strain and customer friction.

Restructured the entire operating cadence by applying behavioral psychology (Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs), authoring standardized field playbooks, and replacing passive review meetings with daily empowerment huddles and gamified "Challenge & Reward" milestones.

  • 48-Hour Sprint Mobilization: Mobilized the entire divisional network to clear 40% of the total monthly target within the first 48 hours of cycle kickoff.
  • Zero Cash-Transit Breaches: Established strict procedural SOPs and daily sign-off registers, maintaining 100% regulatory audit compliance.
  • Frontline Empowerment: Transformed frontline motivation, resulting in a sustained 25% lift in regional lead conversions.
03 / Speech Intelligence & Automation

AI-Powered Call QA Analysis Engine

1,200 Daily Calls Audited
100% Audit Coverage
360h Saved Monthly

Manual QA listening audits covered less than 2% of daily customer interaction recordings across 12 decentralized telecalling units. Agent execution errors, script deviations, and customer churn signals routinely went undetected.

Engineered an automated speech evaluation engine processing 1,200 daily recordings (800 minutes of voice data). The system automatically extracts transcripts, assesses agent objection-handling alignment against structured rubrics, and detects customer purchase intent signals.

  • Complete Audit Coverage: Elevated evaluation coverage from under 2% to 100% across all 12 decentralized operational units.
  • Queuing Matrix Architecture: Built rate-limited queuing pipelines to handle heavy audio ingestion bursts while staying within strict API thresholds.
  • Actionable Coaching Telemetry: Delivered automated daily coaching summaries to team leads, saving 360 hours of manual QA listening every month.
